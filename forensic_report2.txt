======================================================================
PDF RENDERING LAYERS - WHY TEXT IS DOUBLED
======================================================================

PDF renders in this order:
  1. Page Content (static background, borders, our painted rect+text)
  2. Annotations  (form field widgets = pypdf's filled text)

Our white rect + text goes into Layer 1
pypdf's form field text goes into Layer 2 (ON TOP)
RESULT: Both layers visible = double/ghost text!

FIX: After painting, DELETE the widget annotation so only our text remains
